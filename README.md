## About One Piece
**One Piece** is an epic adventure series created by Eiichiro Oda. It follows Monkey D. Luffy and his crew as they sail across the Grand Line in search of the ultimate treasure.

### Quick Facts:
* **Protagonist:** Monkey D. Luffy (Straw Hat Luffy)
* **Goal:** To find the One Piece and become the Pirate King
* **Genre:** Action, Adventure, Fantasy

> *"If you don't take risks, you can't create a future."* — Monkey D. Luffy
